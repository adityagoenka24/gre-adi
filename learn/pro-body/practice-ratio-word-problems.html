Q1 · WRD-RAT-003 — Answer: 45

Step 1 — name k. Alice gets 3k and Bob gets 5k. Step 2 — the invariant here is the total: 3k + 5k = 8k = 120, so k = 15. One ratio part is worth $15. Step 3 — answer what was asked. Alice = 3k = 3 x 15 = $45. Check: Bob = 5 x 15 = $75, and 45 + 75 = $120. Also 45:75 = 3:5. Correct. Answer: 45.

Trap. Dividing 120 by 3 to get 40, or by 5 to get 24. The denominator in a share-a-total problem is always the SUM of the parts, 3 + 5 = 8, never one of the parts on its own.

Q4 · WRD-RAT-009 — Answer: C

Q5 · WRD-RAT-010 — Answer: B

Q15 · WRD-RAT-035 — Answer: D

Both denominators are positive because x > 0, so cross-multiplication is safe and preserves the direction of the comparison. Column A gives (x + 2)(x + 9) = x^2 + 11x + 18. Column B gives (x + 5)(x + 5) = x^2 + 10x + 25. Subtract: (x^2 + 11x + 18) - (x^2 + 10x + 25) = x - 7. Trap. Testing one small value - x = 1 gives 0.500 against 0.600 - and locking in choice B; the 'adding to the top and the bottom drags a fraction toward 1' rule points the same way and feels like confirmation.
