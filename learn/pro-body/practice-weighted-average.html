Q1 · STA-WAV-001 — Answer: B

Step 1 — turn each average into a total. Averages cannot be combined directly; totals can. Group of 10: 10 x 80 = 800. Group of 15: 15 x 70 = 1050. Step 2 — add the totals and divide by the total head count. (800 + 1050)/25 = 1850/25 = 74. Check the pull direction: the 15-student group is larger, so the combined mean must sit below the midpoint 75 and closer to 70. 74 does. Correct. Answer: 74.

Trap. Averaging the averages: (80 + 70)/2 = 75, choice C. That would only be right if the two groups were the same size. Choice D, 76, is the same arithmetic with the weights attached to the wrong groups (15 x 80 + 10 x 70, all over 25).

Q2 · STA-WAV-003 — Answer: 70

Step 1 — the credits are the weights: 1 for the quiz, 2 for the exam, total 3. Step 2 — weighted average = (1 x 90 + 2 x 60)/(1 + 2). = (90 + 120)/3 = 210/3 = 70. Check: the exam carries two thirds of the weight, so the answer must sit two thirds of the way from 90 down toward 60, that is 90 - (2/3)(30) = 70. Correct. Answer: 70.

Trap. Answering 75, the plain average of 90 and 60. The exam counts twice, so the mean is dragged toward 60, not parked halfway. Answering 80 is the same slip with the weights swapped: (2 x 90 + 1 x 60)/3 = 80.

Q3 · STA-WAV-008 — Answer: D

Write the combined mean as m = (n1 a + n2 b)/(n1 + n2), with n1 and n2 both at least 1. Step 1 — lower bound. Every value in the sum is weighted against a and b, and since b > a, replacing b by a would lower the total. So m > a. Step 2 — upper bound. By the same argument, replacing a by b would raise the total, so m < b. Step 3 — therefore a < m < b, strictly, because both weights are strictly positive. A is only true when n1 = n2. B and C are impossible by Steps 1 and 2. E is only true when Group 1 is the larger group, which is not stated. Answer: D.

Trap. Choosing A, the plain average, which assumes equal group sizes. Choosing E is the other live error: the combined mean is closer to whichever group is BIGGER, and nothing here says Group 1 is bigger. Options B and C test the sanity check from the capsule: a weighted average can never escape the interval between the two group means.

Trap. Averaging 60 and 80 to get 70 and choosing C. The groups differ in size 5 to 15, so the midpoint is not the combined mean. Column B is exactly the number that error produces, which is why C is the tempting key.

Q8 · STA-WAV-021 — Answer: 70.5

The two-group formula extends to any number of groups: multiply each mean by its count, add, divide by the total count. Step 1 — group totals: 5 x 60 = 300; 3 x 75 = 225; 2 x 90 = 180. Step 2 — overall total: 300 + 225 + 180 = 705. Step 3 — total students: 5 + 3 + 2 = 10. Step 4 — average: 705/10 = 70.5. Check: the largest group sits at the lowest mean, so the answer must fall below the middle mean of 75. It does. Answer: 70.5.

Trap. Averaging the three group means, (60 + 75 + 90)/3 = 75, which silently gives each group the same weight. Half the students are in the 60 group, so the true mean is dragged well below 75. Weighting by the group MEANS instead of the counts is the other version of the same error.

Q9 · STA-WAV-024 — Answer: A, B, D

M is a weighted average: the weights 3/8 and 5/8 are non-negative and sum to 1. A - the distances are M - a = 5(b - a)/8 and b - M = 3(b - a)/8. When a < b both are positive and the second is smaller, so M is closer to b. TRUE. B - both weights are strictly positive, so M is a strict convex combination of a and b and cannot reach either endpoint unless a = b. TRUE. C - M - (a + b)/2 = (3a + 5b)/8 - (4a + 4b)/8 = (b - a)/8. This is positive only when b > a. Take a = 5, b = 1: M = (15 + 5)/8 = 2.5 while (a + b)/2 = 3, so M is smaller. FALSE. D - adding 4 to both: (3(a + 4) + 5(b + 4))/8 = M + 32/8 = M + 4, because the weights sum to 1. TRUE. E - multiplying both by 3: (3(3a) + 5(3b))/8 = 3M exactly. There is no extra 8. FALSE. Answer: A, B, D.

Trap. C is the trap for anyone who reads 'the heavier weight sits on b' as 'M is always above the midpoint'. That holds only when b is the larger value; reverse the roles and M drops below the midpoint. E baits the student who has just used the shift rule in D and assumes scaling behaves the same way - a weighted average shifts by the shift but scales by the scale factor, with no additive leftover.

Q10 · STA-WAV-027 — Answer: B

Average speed is never a weighted average of the speeds by DISTANCE - it is total distance over total time. Step 1 — let the one-way distance be d. Total distance = 2d. Total time = d/v1 + d/v2. Step 2 — average speed = 2d / (d/v1 + d/v2) = 2/(1/v1 + 1/v2) = 2 v1 v2/(v1 + v2), the harmonic mean. Step 3 — compare with the arithmetic mean. (v1 + v2)/2 - 2 v1 v2/(v1 + v2) = ((v1 + v2)^2 - 4 v1 v2)/(2(v1 + v2)) = (v1 - v2)^2/(2(v1 + v2)). Since v1 is not equal to v2, that numerator is strictly positive and the denominator is positive, so Column B is strictly greater. Concrete check: v1 = 60, v2 = 40 gives average speed 2(60)(40)/100 = 48, while (60 + 40)/2 = 50. Answer: B.

Trap. Choosing C, on the reasoning that half the trip is at each speed so the mean of the speeds must be the average speed. The two halves are equal in DISTANCE, not in TIME - the car spends longer on the slow leg, so the slow speed carries the heavier time weight and drags the true average below the midpoint. A weighted average of rates equals total-over-total only when the weights are the times, that is, the denominators of the rates.

Q12 · STA-WAV-030 — Answer: B, C, D

The class mean is a weighted average of 70 and 85 with strictly positive weights, so it must lie in the OPEN interval from 70 to 85. A - 69 is below 70. No mixture of two groups can produce a mean below the smaller group mean. FALSE. B - 72 is inside the interval; take many boys and few girls, for example 13 boys and 2 girls: (13 x 70 + 2 x 85)/15 = (910 + 170)/15 = 72. TRUE. C - 77.5 is the midpoint, achieved with equal numbers of boys and girls. TRUE. D - 84 is inside the interval; take 1 boy and 14 girls: (70 + 14 x 85)/15 = (70 + 1190)/15 = 84. TRUE.
