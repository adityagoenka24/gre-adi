2. Must-Know Rules

Quadrant x y Example
I (top right) + + (3, 4)
II (top left) - + (-3, 4)
III (bottom left) - - (-3, -4)
IV (bottom right) + - (3, -4)

On the axes:

  • A point on the x-axis has y = 0, e.g. (5, 0).
  • A point on the y-axis has x = 0, e.g. (0, -2).
  • The origin is (0, 0). It belongs to no quadrant.

Reflections:

Reflect across (x, y) becomes
x-axis (x, -y)
y-axis (-x, y)
origin (-x, -y)
line y = x (y, x)

Translations: moving right by a adds a to x; moving up by b adds b to y. So (x, y) -> (x + a, y + b).

Distance from the axes: the distance of (x, y) from the x-axis is |y|, and from the y-axis is |x|.

3. Worked Examples

Example 1 — Quadrant identification

In which quadrant does the point (-6, 2.5) lie?

x is negative, y is positive -> Quadrant II.

Example 2 — Reflection

Point P(4, -7) is reflected across the y-axis. What are the new coordinates?

Reflecting across the y-axis negates x: (-4, -7).

Example 3 — Area from coordinates

A rectangle has vertices (1, 2), (7, 2), (7, 6) and (1, 6). Find its area.

Width = 7 - 1 = 6. Height = 6 - 2 = 4.

Area = 24.

4. GRE Traps

  • Reversing the coordinates. (3, 5) is not (5, 3) unless the point is on y = x.
  • Placing the origin in a quadrant. It is on both axes and in none.
  • Reflection across the wrong axis. Reflecting across the x-axis changes the y sign — the axis you reflect across is the one that stays fixed.
  • Assuming a point with a negative coordinate is in Quadrant III. Only if both are negative.
  • Distance versus coordinate. The distance from the x-axis is |y|, always non-negative.
  • Assuming the figure is drawn to scale on a coordinate grid where the problem says otherwise.

5. Speed Tricks

  • Sketch it. A rough set of axes with the points marked takes 10 seconds and prevents sign errors.
  • For rectangles aligned to the axes, subtract coordinates directly to get side lengths.
  • Signs by quadrant: (+,+), (-,+), (-,-), (+,-) going anticlockwise. Chant it.
  • Reflection mnemonic: "across the x-axis, x stays".
  • For area of a triangle with axis-parallel sides, use the two legs as base and height.

6. Self-Check

Q1. In which quadrant is (5, -3)?

Q2. Reflect (-2, 6) across the x-axis.

Q3. A triangle has vertices (0,0), (6,0) and (0,8). Find its area.

Answers

A1. Quadrant IV.

A2. (-2, -6).

A3. Legs 6 and 8 along the axes: (1/2)(6)(8) = 24.

7. One-Line Summary for the Board

x first, y second. Quadrants run anticlockwise from top right. Across the x-axis, x stays.
