Bank code: ARI-NLO 30 questions Pro

Bank code: ARI-NLO · Section: Arithmetic · 30 questions — Easy 5 · Medium 8 · Hard 12 · Extreme 5

Right on the number line is greater; for negatives, closer to zero wins - and whenever a variable is unpinned, test x < -1, -1 < x < 0, 0 < x < 1 and x > 1 before you answer.

Every question below is live in the question bank under the ID shown — the sheet and the portal are the same questions. Attempt a level with the clock running, then check Part C.

Part A — Questions

Quantitative Comparison — the four choices are always the same, so they are not reprinted: (A) Column A is greater · (B) Column B is greater · (C) The two quantities are equal · (D) The relationship cannot be determined from the information given
