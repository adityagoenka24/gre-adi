Bank code: NUM-REM · Section: Arithmetic · 30 questions — Easy 5 · Medium 8 · Hard 12 · Extreme 5

Write N = dq + r, reduce at every step instead of at the end, and remember that powers of a fixed base repeat with a period of 4 or less.
