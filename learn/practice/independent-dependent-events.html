Independent & Dependent Events — Practice Set

Bank code: STA-IDE 30 questions Pro

Bank code: STA-IDE · Section: statistics · 30 questions — Easy 6 · Medium 11 · Hard 12 · Extreme 1

Ask one question of every multi-stage problem: after the first event happens, has the pool changed? If yes the events are dependent and you must update both numbers; if no they are independent and you simply multiply.

Every question below is live in the question bank under the ID shown — the sheet and the portal are the same questions. Attempt a level with the clock running, then check Part C.

Part A — Questions

Quantitative Comparison — the four choices are always the same, so they are not reprinted: (A) Quantity A is greater · (B) Quantity B is greater · (C) The two quantities are equal · (D) The relationship cannot be determined from the information given

(C) They are not independent, because P(B | A) is not equal to P(A)
(D) They are not independent, because P(A and B) = 0.2, which is not 0
(E) Independence cannot be decided without being told P(B)
