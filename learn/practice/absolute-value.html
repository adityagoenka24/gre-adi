Bank code: NUM-ABS · Section: Arithmetic · 30 questions — Easy 5 · Medium 8 · Hard 12 · Extreme 5

|x| means distance from zero: equations split into two cases, less-than gives the inside of an interval and greater-than gives the two rays outside it.

Every question below is live in the question bank under the ID shown — the sheet and the portal are the same questions. Attempt a level with the clock running, then check Part C.
