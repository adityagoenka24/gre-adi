Bank code: NUM-PRF · Section: Number Properties · 30 questions — Easy 5 · Medium 8 · Hard 12 · Extreme 5

1 is not prime and 2 is the only even prime - prime factorise first, because divisibility, GCD, LCM and factor counts all fall straight out of the exponents.

Every question below is live in the question bank under the ID shown — the sheet and the portal are the same questions. Attempt a level with the clock running, then check Part C.
