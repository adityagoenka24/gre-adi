Percentage Word Problems — Practice Set

Bank code: WRD-PCT 30 questions Pro

Bank code: WRD-PCT · Section: Word Problems · 30 questions — Easy 5 · Medium 17 · Hard 8

Find the base before you find the percent: "percent of the remainder" is not "percent of the original", and "percent of the subgroup" is not "percent of the total".

Every question below is live in the question bank under the ID shown — the sheet and the portal are the same questions. Attempt a level with the clock running, then check Part C.

Part A — Questions

Quantitative Comparison — the four choices are always the same, so they are not reprinted: (A) Quantity A is greater · (B) Quantity B is greater · (C) The two quantities are equal · (D) The relationship cannot be determined from the information given

(A) 1,650
(B) 1,800
(C) 2,250
(D) 2,750
(E) 3,000

(C) $3,660
(D) $6,300
(E) $9,200

(C) 12 percent of the books sold were paperback textbooks.
(D) 30 percent of the books sold were textbooks.

(C) He spends more than twice as much on rent as on food.

Q25 · WRD-PCT-061 · Select all that apply · 110s

A firm has 400 employees. 60 percent of them work in sales and the rest work in operations. 45 percent of the sales employees and 70 percent of the operations employees have been with the firm for more than five years. Which of the following are true? Select all that apply.

(A) More operations employees than sales employees have been with the firm for more than five years.
(B) 55 percent of the firm's employees have been with the firm for more than five years.
(C) 57.5 percent of the firm's employees have been with the firm for more than five years.
(D) If the firm hired 40 more operations employees, each of whom had already been with the firm for more than five years, then more than 60 percent of its employees would have more than five years of service.
(E) 108 sales employees have been with the firm for more than five years.

Q26 · WRD-PCT-062 · Select all that apply · 105s

(A) 105 of the women work in the London office.
(B) 80 of the men do not work in the London office.
(C) 35 percent of the women work in the London office.
(D) Women are a majority of the employees who work in the London office.
(E) 65 percent of the employees who do not work in the London office are women.

Q27 · WRD-PCT-063 · MCQ · 105s

In 2024 it received 2,400 applications; its offer rate was 10 percentage points lower than in 2023, and the percent of those offered a place who enrolled was 10 percentage points higher than in 2023. By what percent did the number of students who enrolled change from 2023 to 2024?

(A) It rose by 20%.
(B) It rose by 8%.
(C) It did not change.
(D) It fell by 2%.
(E) It fell by 10%.

Q30 · WRD-PCT-067 · Select all that apply · 110s

A shop's revenue comes only from books and stationery. Last month books accounted for 60 percent of its revenue. This month book revenue fell by 20 percent while stationery revenue was unchanged. Which of the following must be true? Select all that apply.

(A) Total revenue fell by 12 percent.
(B) Books now account for more than 55 percent of revenue.
(C) If book revenue were to fall by a further 25 percent from its present level while stationery revenue stayed where it is, stationery would then account for more than half of revenue.
(D) Book revenue must rise by 25 percent from its present level for total revenue to return to last month's level, assuming stationery revenue stays where it is.
(E) If book revenue returns to last month's level while stationery revenue falls by 10 percent, total revenue will be back at last month's level.
