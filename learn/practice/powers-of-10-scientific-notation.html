Bank code: ARI-P10 · Section: Arithmetic · 30 questions — Easy 5 · Medium 8 · Hard 12 · Extreme 5

Separate the digits from the size: compare exponents first, and renormalise so the coefficient sits between 1 and 10.

Every question below is live in the question bank under the ID shown — the sheet and the portal are the same questions. Attempt a level with the clock running, then check Part C.
