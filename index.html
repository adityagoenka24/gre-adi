Real question from the bank — Extreme Hard
A store raises a price by 20%, then offers a 20% discount on the new price, then raises it again by 25%. If the final price is ₹810, what was the original price?
A. ₹648 B. ₹660 C. ₹675 ✓ D. ₹720
Explanation: Multiply the three multipliers: 1.20 × 0.80 × 1.25 = 1.20. So Original × 1.20 = 810, giving Original = 810 ÷ 1.20 = ₹675.
Named trapStudents add +20% − 20% + 25% = +25% and compute 810 ÷ 1.25 = ₹648 (Option A). Successive percent changes must be multiplied, not added — each applies to the previous price, not the original.
