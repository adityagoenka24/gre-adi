What is the ETS Diagnostic Service, and where do I find my report?

After you take the GRE, ETS gives every test-taker a free GRE Diagnostic Service report in their ETS account. It lists every Quant question you saw with its type, whether it was pure maths or a real-life setting, whether you got it right, how hard it was on a 1–5 scale, and exactly how long you spent on it. Open the Quantitative Reasoning page, save or screenshot it, and drop it above.

Haven't taken the GRE yet? Take our free full mock instead — it produces its own diagnosis at the end.

Is my data safe?

Yes, and not as a promise — as a fact about how this is built. Your report is read entirely inside your own browser using on-device text recognition. It is never uploaded, never stored, and never sent to any server, including ours. The ETS report has your name printed in its header; that name is never extracted or transmitted. Close the tab and nothing remains.
