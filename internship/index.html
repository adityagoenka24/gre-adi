FAQ

Good to know

Do I need to install anything?
No. The SQL engine (DuckDB-WASM) runs entirely inside your browser tab. You just need a modern browser and an internet connection to load it once.
What happens right after I pay?
You'll get an email confirming your lifetime access along with instructions for signing in to the app with your access details. If you don't hear back within a day, reply to the confirmation email.
Is my progress saved?
Yes — your progress syncs automatically once you're signed in, and you can also export/import a progress file at any time as a backup.
Can I use this on a phone or tablet?
It works best on a laptop or desktop where you can comfortably write SQL and view result tables. A tablet with a keyboard is usable; a phone is not recommended.
Is this really a one-time payment?
Yes — ₹1990 once, and your access doesn't expire. There's no subscription, no recurring charge, and nothing to cancel.
