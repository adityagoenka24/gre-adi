The Question Audit · October 2026

Every question we deleted,
and the reason we deleted it.

You cannot verify a stranger's test score. You can verify whether they throw away their own work when it isn't good enough. In October 2026 every question in the bank was re-scored against a written ETS-fidelity rubric. 370 failed and were deleted. Nothing was generated to replace them, and the bank got smaller. This page is the complete list.

What the audit checked

Every question was read against a written rubric covering stem register, distractor design, whether the figure matches the stem, whether the explanation derives the keyed answer, whether the named trap lands on an option that exists, and whether the difficulty label matches the work the question actually demands. Each question was scored 1–5 for fidelity to real ETS style.

Anything scoring 2 or below was deleted. So was anything carrying a structural defect at any score — a wrong key, an explanation contradicting its own answer, a figure disagreeing with its stem, a corrupted chart, a malformed question type. A wrong practice question is worse than no practice question, because it teaches you to distrust your own correct reasoning.

What the failure reasons mean

explanation_error The worked solution contradicts the keyed answer.
wrong_key The keyed answer was simply wrong.
no_correct_option None of the options was correct.
multiple_correct More than one option was correct in a single-answer question.
filler_distractors Wrong options were padding — an arithmetic run around the key — rather than real errors a student makes.
trap_mismatch The named trap described an error that does not produce any option shown.
difficulty_mislabelled Labelled at a band the question does not earn.
ambiguous_stem The wording admitted more than one defensible answer.
figure_missing The question referred to a figure that was not there.
figure_contradicts_stem The drawing disagreed with the numbers in the stem.
chart_data_corrupt The chart plotted different data from the one the stem described.
missing_data The stem omitted a value its own solution required.
malformed_type Stored as the wrong question type — a comparison saved as multiple choice, say.
degenerate_select_all A select-all where every option was correct, or only one was.
duplicate_stem The same question already existed elsewhere in the bank.
textbook_phrasing Written like a textbook exercise — "Simplify:", "Find x" — not like an ETS item.
terminology_nonets Used terms the real exam does not, such as "Column A".
qc_column_wording Pre-2011 comparison wording.
explanation_offsyllabus Solved by a method the GRE does not test, such as logarithms or the law of cosines.
grindy_computation Difficulty came from tedious arithmetic rather than an idea.
ugly_numbers Numbers the real exam would never choose.
telegraphed_method The stem named the technique and gave the question away.
contrived_context A cover story no ETS item would use.
