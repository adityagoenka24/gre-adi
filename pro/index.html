Help

Common questions

Stuck? Coach Aditya replies personally, usually within 2 hours.

How do I log in to the Pro app?
+

Open the Pro app and enter the email you used at payment. No password needed. If your email isn't recognised, it may still be in the 1–2 hour activation window — email goenka.aditya.kol@gmail.com if it takes longer.

Which mock should I take first?
+

Start with Mock 1 or 2 (Balanced, 160-level) to establish a baseline, then move up the ladder — Moderate (162), Moderate-Hard (164), Hard (166), and finally Very Hard / Expert (168–170) as your accuracy improves.

How does Send to Coach work?
+

After a session or mock, use the Send to Coach button — it emails your full session data to Coach Aditya. Pro + Coach members get a personalised review within 24 hours; Pro members can use it for occasional check-ins.

Does the app work offline?
+

Yes — after the first load, the practice app works offline in your browser. Full mocks and Send to Coach need a connection.
