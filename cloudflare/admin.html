📦 Pro Question Files

The paid question bank and the paid mocks are no longer published on the website. They sit in Cloudflare KV and the Worker hands them only to signed-in students on a registered device whose plan covers the file. Use this to put them there, and again whenever you change a file.

  1. Save your Worker URL and admin secret at the top of this page.
  2. Click Choose folder below and pick the pro folder of your repo. Only bank/ and mocks/ JSON files are read; everything else in it is ignored.
  3. Click Upload. Files that have not changed since your last upload in this browser are skipped, so a normal update writes only what you edited.

Each uploaded file is one KV write and Cloudflare's free plan allows 1,000 writes a day, so a full upload of about 140 files is fine but do not repeat it dozens of times in a day. Students may see the old copy of a changed file for up to 6 hours (their browser keeps it).
