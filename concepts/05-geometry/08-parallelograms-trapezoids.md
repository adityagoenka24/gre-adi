# Parallelograms, Rhombuses & Trapezoids

**Section:** Geometry  |  **Bank code:** GEO-PTZ  |  **GRE frequency:** Medium

---

## 1. Core Idea

- **Parallelogram:** both pairs of opposite sides parallel.
- **Rhombus:** a parallelogram with all four sides equal.
- **Trapezoid:** exactly one pair of parallel sides.

In every one of them, the **height is the perpendicular distance between the parallel sides** — never the slanted side. That single confusion accounts for most errors in this topic.

---

## 2. Must-Know Rules

| Figure | Area | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Parallelogram | base x height | height is perpendicular |
| Rhombus | base x height, or (d1 x d2)/2 | diagonals are perpendicular |
| Trapezoid | (1/2)(a + b) x h | a, b are the parallel sides |
| Kite | (d1 x d2)/2 | diagonals perpendicular |

**Parallelogram properties:**

- Opposite sides equal and parallel
- Opposite angles equal
- Consecutive angles supplementary (sum to 180)
- Diagonals bisect each other (but are **not** equal)
- Each diagonal splits it into two congruent triangles

**Rhombus properties:** all parallelogram properties, plus

- All four sides equal
- Diagonals are **perpendicular** and bisect the vertex angles
- The diagonals cut the rhombus into four congruent right triangles

**Trapezoid.** The area formula is "average of the parallel sides times the height". An **isosceles trapezoid** has equal non-parallel sides, equal base angles, and equal diagonals.

**The median (midsegment) of a trapezoid** joins the midpoints of the non-parallel sides and equals (a + b)/2.

## 3. Worked Examples

### Example 1 — Parallelogram area

*A parallelogram has base 12 and a slanted side of 8, with the angle between them 30 degrees. Find its area.*

The height is not 8; it is the perpendicular component.

In the 30-60-90 triangle formed, the side opposite 30 is half the hypotenuse: h = 8/2 = 4.

Area = 12 x 4 = **48**.

### Example 2 — Rhombus from diagonals

*A rhombus has diagonals of 10 and 24. Find its area and its side length.*

Area = (10 x 24)/2 = **120**.

The diagonals bisect each other perpendicularly, giving right triangles with legs 5 and 12.

Side = sqrt(25 + 144) = **13**.

### Example 3 — Trapezoid

*A trapezoid has parallel sides 7 and 13 and height 6. Find its area.*

Area = (1/2)(7 + 13)(6) = (1/2)(20)(6) = **60**.

---

## 4. GRE Traps

- **Using the slanted side as the height.** The height is always perpendicular to the base.
- **Assuming a parallelogram's diagonals are equal.** They bisect each other but are equal only in a rectangle.
- **Assuming diagonals are perpendicular.** True in a rhombus and a kite, not in a general parallelogram.
- **Using the trapezoid formula with a non-parallel side** as a or b.
- **Forgetting the 1/2 in the trapezoid area.**
- **Assuming a rhombus is a square.** It has equal sides but not necessarily right angles.
- **Adding the diagonals instead of multiplying** in the rhombus area formula.

---

## 5. Speed Tricks

- **Rhombus area = half the product of the diagonals.** Faster than base x height when the diagonals are given.
- **Trapezoid area = average of the parallel sides x height.** Say it that way; the formula sticks.
- **Any parallelogram can be cut and rearranged into a rectangle** of the same base and height — a useful board demonstration.
- **The diagonals of a rhombus create four identical right triangles** with legs equal to the half-diagonals. Pythagoras gives the side instantly.
- **Consecutive angles supplementary** — if one angle of a parallelogram is 110, the next is 70, and the opposite is 110.

## 6. Self-Check

**Q1.** A parallelogram has base 15 and height 8. Find its area.

**Q2.** A rhombus has diagonals 16 and 30. Find its side length.

**Q3.** A trapezoid has area 90, height 9, and one parallel side 8. Find the other parallel side.

<details>
<summary>Answers</summary>

**A1.** 15 x 8 = **120**.

**A2.** Half-diagonals 8 and 15; side = sqrt(64 + 225) = **17**.

**A3.** 90 = (1/2)(8 + b)(9) -> 20 = 8 + b -> **b = 12**.

</details>

## 7. One-Line Summary for the Board
