A store raises a price by 20%, then offers a 20% discount on the new price, then raises it again by 25%. If the final price is $810, what was the original price?
Explanation. Multiply the three multipliers rather than adding the percentages: 1.20 × 0.80 × 1.25 = 1.20. So Original × 1.20 = 810, giving Original = 810 ÷ 1.20 = $675.
Named trapStudents add +20% − 20% + 25% = +25% and compute 810 ÷ 1.25 = $648, which is option A. Successive percent changes must be multiplied, not added — each one applies to the previous price, not to the original.
