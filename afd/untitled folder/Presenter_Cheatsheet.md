# PharmaBoard — Presenter Cheatsheet

**For:** Quarterly Sales Meet · VPs, National Sales Leaders, Regional Managers, Marketing Heads, MSRs
**How to use:** Read top-to-bottom in the order tabs are arranged. Each card below = the order on screen.

---

## Quick navigation tricks (good to know on stage)

| Action | How |
|---|---|
| Open any chart fullscreen | Click **Expand** on the chart card → zoom/pan/reset with the toolbar |
| Lock dashboard to a month | Click a bar on **Revenue Momentum** — everything else reflows to that month |
| Lock dashboard to a product/customer/territory | Click that element in any clickable chart — Pulse, Growth, Opportunity, Quadrant |
| Clear the lock | Click **Clear ✕** in the blue bar at the top |
| Save current view | Click **Export Brief** in the top right — generates a printable HTML brief |

## TAB 01 · EXECUTIVE PULSE

> **Lead line:** *"This single screen is the answer to 'how are we doing?' — net, growth, drag, coverage."*

### Hero KPI row (4 dark tiles)
| Tile | What it shows | Story to tell |
|---|---|---|
| **Net Revenue** | Filtered net sales total + latest month + sparkline | "We're at X across the period. The latest month is Y." |
| **Growth (MoM)** | Month-over-month change of net revenue | "Direction matters more than scale — we're trending [up/flat/down]." |
| **Return Drag** | Returns as % of gross | "X% of gross is leaking back. Healthy is <2%. We're at [Y]." |
| **Doctor Coverage** *(only with Doctor Master)* | Active mapped doctors / total mapped | "X% of our targeted doctor base is actually generating revenue." |

### Intelligence Brief (4–8 colored cards)
Auto-generated insights based on the data. Each is tagged **Growth · Risk · Watch · Focus · Opportunity** with a one-line explanation. **These ARE the headlines — read them aloud.**

### Revenue Momentum & Return Drag
- **What:** Monthly net (blue bars) and returns (red bars) with return-rate line (amber)
- **Read:** Are bars climbing? Is the line creeping up?
- **Story:** *"Look at month 7 — we shipped record revenue but return rate jumped 2 points. We need to talk about that batch."*
- **Trick:** Click a month → entire dashboard locks to it

### Latest-Month Growth Bridge (Waterfall)
- **What:** Top product contributors to MoM change. Green = added revenue, red = lost revenue
- **Read:** Three green at the left and red at the right = healthy. All red = problem.
- **Story:** *"This bar tells you which 3 products drove the quarter — and which 2 are dragging us back."*

### Revenue Concentration (Pareto)
- **What:** Top 30 customers ranked, with cumulative-% line and 80% threshold
- **Read:** Where the line crosses 80% = how many customers carry 80% of revenue
- **Story:** *"Top 10 customers = X% of revenue. If they go, we go. Protection program is non-negotiable."*
- **Threshold:** Pareto-healthy = ~50%; pareto-risky = >75% in top 10

### Territory Opportunity Matrix
- **What:** Each bubble = a territory. X-axis = net revenue, Y-axis = MoM growth %, size = customer count, color = return rate (green good, red bad)
- **Read:** Top-right = scale + growth (replicate). Bottom-right = decline despite scale (intervene). Top-left = small but growing (invest). Bottom-left = small + declining (deprioritize).
- **Story:** *"These four territories deserve a regional-head site visit this quarter."*

### Strategic Decisions for Leadership
6 colored cards generated from the data: **Protect · Accelerate · Fix · Intervene · Realign · Expand**. Each names a customer, product, MSR or territory and tells you the action.

## TAB 02 · GROWTH ENGINE

> **Lead line:** *"Now let's decompose growth — what's driving us, what's pulling us back."*

### Growth KPI row (5 cards)
- **Latest Month Net** · **MoM Δ** · **Accelerating SKUs** · **Declining SKUs** · **New / Lost**
- "We added X new SKUs to the line and lost Y."

### Product Growth Contribution (Waterfall)
- **What:** Same as Pulse but with more product depth — 14 bars instead of 10
- **Story:** *"Of our top-10 products, 6 grew and 4 declined. Net was +₹X."*

### Product Momentum Matrix
- **What:** X = net revenue (log scale), Y = growth %, size = customers, color = return rate
- **Read:** Top-right quadrant = stars (scale + growth). Bottom-right = need attention (scale but declining). Top-left = rising stars. Bottom-left = harvest or retire.
- **Story:** *"These three names in the top-right are our next quarter's headlines. Detail them harder."*

### Top Customer × Product Heatmap
- **What:** 15 customers × 12 products with revenue intensity
- **Read:** Dark cells = high revenue concentration in that relationship
- **Story:** *"The darkest cell shows our most valuable single relationship. We have an account plan in place."*

### Accelerating / Declining Tables
- Side-by-side. **Accelerating** = growth opportunity. **Declining** = intervention candidates with return-rate context.
- **Story:** *"Top of the left table = our growth plan. Top of the right table = our defense plan."*

## TAB 03 · FIELD FORCE (MSR + DOCTOR) — *the heart of the deck*

> **Lead line:** *"Now we look at the people executing the strategy and the doctor portfolio they're playing."*
> If you see "Doctor Master not loaded" banner, click **+ Doctor Master** in the top bar first.

### Field Force KPI row (6 cards)
- **Active MSRs · Net Revenue · Avg per MSR · Top Performer · Doctor Coverage · Targeting Effectiveness**
- "X% of our revenue is on-strategy (from products mapped to our doctors). We want this above 70%."

### MSR Performance Scorecard
- **What:** Every rep with a 0-100 **Composite Score** combining: revenue (40) + growth (25) + reach (15) + breadth (10) + targeting (10)
- **Color codes:** Score badge = green if >50, blue if 30–50, amber if <30. Targeting = green if ≥70%, amber 40–70%, red <40%.
- **Story:** *"This is the only ranking we use. It's a multi-dimensional score, not just revenue. Anyone above 50 is a top performer."*
- **Sort the table** by any column. Click headers.

### Doctor Portfolio Pyramid
- **What:** Each MSR's doctors split into **SUPER CORE (violet)**, **VIP (gold)**, **CORE (blue)**
- **Read:** Reps with a balanced pyramid have a healthy long-term portfolio. Reps with all-blue (only CORE) have no premium pipeline. Reps with all-gold but low revenue have execution failure.
- **Story:** *"[MSR Name] has [N] VIP doctors but revenue from that territory is flat — that's a coaching conversation."*

### Targeting Effectiveness
- **What:** Horizontal bars showing % of each MSR's revenue from products mapped to their assigned doctors. Green ≥70%, amber 40–70%, red <40%. Dashed line = 70% target.
- **Story:** *"Half our reps are above the target line. The other half are selling whatever moves — not what we asked them to push. We're losing strategic alignment in the field."*
- **Most important insight on this tab.**

### Speciality Revenue Mix
- **What:** Revenue apportioned by the speciality mix of doctors in each city
- **Story:** *"Cardiologists and Orthopaedicians anchor [X]% of revenue. We're under-monetised on Gynaecologists relative to our doctor base — that's marketing's brief."*

### HQ vs Extension Productivity
- **What:** Revenue (bars) and ₹/doctor (line) for HQ vs Extension territories
- **Read:** If HQ revenue/doctor is much higher than EX, reps are coasting on home turf. EX days are how growth happens.
- **Story:** *"HQ productivity is X, EX is Y. If they're nearly equal we have a great extension culture. If HQ is 3× EX, our reps aren't doing extension days."*

### Doctor Watchlist
- **What:** MSR × City hotspots tagged: **Anchor (gold)** · **Untapped (violet)** · **Diversify (amber)** · **Performer (green)**
- **Read:** **Untapped rows are gold** — high VIP density but low revenue = the next growth dollar.
- **Story:** *"This row right here — [MSR] in [city] has [N] VIP doctors and only [X] revenue. That's the easiest ₹50 L in this dashboard."*

## TAB 04 · PRODUCT STRATEGY

> **Lead line:** *"Now let's look at the product portfolio — what to back, what to fix, what to harvest."*

### KPI row (5 cards)
- **Active SKUs · Top SKU Share · Accelerating · Declining · High-return SKUs**

### Portfolio Quadrant
- **What:** X = customer reach, Y = months sold (consistency), size = revenue, color = growth (green up, red down)
- **Read:** Top-right (high reach + consistent + green) = portfolio stars. Bottom-left + red = candidates for delisting.
- **Story:** *"These three products are our pillars — they reach many customers consistently with growth. We invest detailing time here."*

### Concentration Risk
- **What:** Top-customer share % per product. Red bars = >80% concentration
- **Story:** *"If this product's top customer walks, we lose [X]% of revenue overnight. We need to broaden the base — or lock the contract."*

### Top Product Monthly Trend
- **What:** Lines for top 8 products over time
- **Story:** *"Three lines are climbing, two are flat, three are sliding. The slides are concerning if they're our scale SKUs."*

### Product Decision Table
- **What:** Every product with role auto-classified: **Accelerate · Defend · Fix · Audit · Harvest · Hold**
- **Story:** *"Each row tells marketing and field exactly what posture to take with this SKU."*
- Sort by any column.

## TAB 05 · TERRITORY

> **Lead line:** *"Now let's go geography — where do we have anomalies?"*

### KPI row (5 cards)
- **Active Territories · Top Territory · Growing · Declining · Quality Concern**

### Risk × Growth Matrix
- **What:** X = growth %, Y = return rate %, size = net revenue
- **Read:** Top-right = high return & high growth (quality concern despite scale). Bottom-right = best (high growth, low returns).
- **Story:** *"This territory is growing but returns are spiking — we have a distribution quality issue, not a sales issue."*

### State-Product Mix Heatmap
- **What:** State (X) × Product (Y) with darker cells = higher revenue
- **Story:** *"Bihar has zero penetration of [Product] but Bengal is strong on it — we have a distribution gap to fix."*

### Top-8 Territory Monthly Trend
- **Story:** *"This territory was our biggest until Q2 — now [other] is overtaking. Need to understand why."*

### Territory Action Table
- **What:** Each territory auto-tagged: **Scale · Intervene · Quality fix · Diversify · Protect · Maintain**
- **Story:** *"Regional managers leave this meeting with this exact list of priorities."*

## Top 5 questions you'll likely get — and the answers in this dashboard

| Question | Where to point |
|---|---|
| "How are we doing?" | Pulse → Hero KPI row + Intelligence Brief |
| "What's the biggest risk?" | Pulse → Risk-tagged insight + Concentration Pareto |
| "Who's my best MSR?" | Field Force → MSR Scorecard sorted by Score |
| "Where's the next ₹50 L?" | Field Force → Doctor Watchlist → Untapped rows |
| "What products do we kill?" | Product Strategy → Decision Table → "Fix" / "Harvest" rows |

---

## The four sentences that should anchor your talk

1. **"We did X across the period, with [direction] momentum."** *(Pulse KPI)*
2. **"Three things drove growth, three things dragged it."** *(Growth Bridge)*
3. **"Our field force is executing the strategy at X% — that needs to be 70%+."** *(Targeting Effectiveness)*
4. **"Here are the priority territories, products, and customers for next quarter."** *(Decision cards + action tables)*

If you say those four things clearly, with the dashboard backing each one up, the meeting is a success.
